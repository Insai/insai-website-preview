Inside Workbench

A connected workspace for biosignal workflows.

  1. Fast · Signal viewer

    Built for power users.

    View any recording in your browser. Instantly.

    • Opens in seconds. Streams from the cloud. Nothing to install.
    • Hotkeys for everything. Step, score and mark without the mouse.
    • Any device. PSG and wearable EEG now, EMG and ECG next.
    Workbench · scrolling a night with the arrow key
  2. Assisted · Analysis

    Expert analysis, at AI speed.

    AI does the grunt work. You do the science.

    • Find it. Hours of signal, narrowed to the segments that need your eye.
    • Check it. Every suggestion shows the signal and checks behind it.
    • Compare it. Run several models and see where they disagree.
    Workbench · reviewing AI marks with the keyboard
  3. Shared · Workspace

    Analyze together, from anywhere.

    Every recording, analysis and mark in one shared workspace.

    • Organize. Projects, linked devices and reports in one place.
    • Collaborate. Invite your team, assign recordings and review the work.
    • Track. Every mark shows who made it and when, person or agent.

    Private and secure. Recordings stored in the EU, identifiers stripped on upload, encrypted at rest, invite only.

    Workbench · a project, a two-device night, a blind task
  4. Agentic · Workflows Early access

    Your agents, fluent in biosignals.

    A skill stack and MCP server for Claude Code and Codex. Agents clean signals, run detectors and test models. Your team reviews every result.

    • Connect it. One command adds Workbench to Claude Code or Codex.
    • Equip it. Skills from preprocessing and QC to events and labels.
    • Extend it. Add your own models, detectors and skills.
    terminal · Claude Code with the Nexus harness
    $
    nexus · MCP connected · 10 skills · 9 tools
    >
    list_recordingsPilot nights · 8 nights, 11 recordings
    inspect_edf8 files · 6 EEG, 2 EOG, 1 EMG
    check_signal_qualityNight 4 · Fp2 noisy, 41 epochs flagged
    compare_hypnogramsyour scoring vs the model · 84 epochs differ
    compare_eventsspindles · 318 differ
    Done. Night 4 needs a look before scoring. The 84 epochs and 318 spindles that differ are in diffs.csv for your team to review.
    In early access. Skills and commands may change. Ask for early access →
