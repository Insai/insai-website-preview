The Nexus platform

Sleep analysis that shows its work.

A night in a lab tells you about one night. Nexus analyzes every night your participants sleep at home, and every number it returns comes with the method behind it.

AnalyzeA full night in detail Every 30 seconds of the night is staged, so you see the cycles and how often sleep broke up. Each stage carries a confidence score, and the raw signal is one click away.
TrackMany nights for context One bad night tells you nothing. Each participant is read against their own baseline across the study, so a real change stands out from a restless one.
DefendAnalytics you can trust Every result shows the method behind it and how clean the signal was. We match published methods to 1e-3, so a reviewer can rerun the analysis and get the same answer.
